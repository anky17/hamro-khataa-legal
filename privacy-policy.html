Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2025

Hamro Khataa ("we", "our", or "the app") is built to help small shopkeepers manage their credit and payment records. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account info — your name and email address, collected when you sign in with Google, used only to identify your account.
  • Business data — customer names, phone numbers, and transaction records you enter into the app.
  • Device storage — data is stored locally on your device using SQLite and optionally synced to Firebase Firestore for backup.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to provide the app's core functionality:

  • Authenticate your identity via Google Sign-In
  • Store and display your customer and transaction records
  • Back up your data to the cloud so it is available across devices

We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

  • Local: SQLite database on your device
  • Cloud: Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud), secured by Firebase Authentication — only you can access your data

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app (Settings → Delete Account) or by visiting our data deletion page.

After deletion, no data is retained. There is no retention period.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In — for authentication (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase / Firestore — for cloud data storage (Firebase Privacy)

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Effective date" at the top will reflect the latest revision.
