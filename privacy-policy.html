Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Hamro Khataa ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under applicable laws including Nepal's Individual Privacy Act, 2075.

1. Information We Collect

We collect and process the following information:

  • Account Information - When you sign in with Google, we collect your name, email address, and Google user ID to identify your account and enable cloud sync. Guest users remain fully anonymous.
  • Shop Ledger Data - Customer names, phone numbers, transaction amounts, dates, and remarks you record in Shop Mode are stored locally on your device using SQLite and, for Google Sign-In users, backed up to Firebase Firestore.
  • Transaction Photos (Camera & Storage) - When you attach a photo of a bill, receipt, or physical ledger entry to a transaction, the image is saved locally on your device storage. Transaction photos are never uploaded to Firebase Firestore cloud servers; they remain strictly on-device unless you manually export an on-device backup file (.hkbackup).
  • Contacts Access (Optional) - In Shop Mode, you may optionally use the device contact picker to select an existing contact's name and phone number to prefill customer details. We only read the contact you explicitly choose; we never read, collect, or upload your entire address book.
  • Personal Expense & Budget Data - If you use Personal Mode (income and expense tracking, custom categories, monthly budgets, category limits, and payment methods/wallets), all such entries and preferences are stored exclusively on your device using SQLite and local device storage. This data is never synced to Firebase or any external server, for guest and Google Sign-In users alike.
  • App Analytics & Crash Diagnostics - To monitor app stability, diagnose bugs, and improve user experience, we collect aggregated, privacy-safe analytics events and diagnostic crash reports via Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. We never collect financial transaction amounts, customer names, phone numbers, or private ledger remarks in analytics.
  • Device Token - When you grant notification permission, we collect a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token to deliver app notifications and reminders. This token is not linked to your personal identity.
  • In-App Announcements - We may deliver announcements, tips, or update notices via Firebase Firestore. No personal data is collected through this feature.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Display your business ledger, customer balances, and transaction history
  • Sync and back up your Shop Mode ledger data to Firebase Firestore (Google Sign-In users only)
  • Calculate personal budgets, daily Safe to Spend amounts, weekly summaries, and spending reports locally on your device
  • Enable you to generate PDF statements and prefilled WhatsApp reminders that you review and share manually
  • Create and restore user-initiated on-device backup archives (.hkbackup)
  • Identify your account across devices upon sign-in
  • Diagnose app crashes and monitor performance via Firebase Crashlytics to prevent data loss and resolve stability issues
  • Understand aggregated feature usage to make the app more reliable and easier to use
  • Send scheduled reminders and deliver in-app announcements

We do not sell, rent, share, or monetise your personal information in any way.

3. Data Storage, Backups & Security

Local data is stored securely on your device via SQLite and local app storage. Cloud data (for Shop Mode ledger on Google Sign-In accounts) is stored in Google Firebase Firestore, protected by Google's security infrastructure and access rules restricting data strictly to your authenticated account.

Personal Mode Isolation: Your personal income, expense records, budgets, category limits, and payment methods are stored locally only and are never uploaded to Firebase Firestore cloud storage.

On-Device Backup Archives (.hkbackup): Hamro Khataa includes an on-device backup and restore utility. When you choose "Backup to Device", the app packages your database records, personal preferences, and transaction receipt photos into a compressed archive file. You decide where to save or share this file (such as your local device storage or personal cloud drive).

We do not store payment card information, bank account credentials, or payment passwords of any kind.

4. Cross-Border Data Transfer

By using Firebase services, your business/ledger data (for Google Sign-In users) may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside Nepal, including in the United States, where Google LLC operates its infrastructure.

This transfer is necessary to provide cloud sync and backup functionality. Google's data handling is governed by its own Privacy Policy and complies with international data protection standards.

Your personal income/expense tracker data never leaves your device, so no cross-border transfer applies to it.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In - for user authentication
  • Firebase Firestore - for cloud ledger data storage (Google users only)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) - for push notifications
  • Firebase Crashlytics - for crash reporting, stability monitoring, and bug diagnosis
  • Firebase Analytics - for privacy-safe, aggregated app feature usage measurement (without financial figures or personal identifiers)

These services are governed by Google LLC's Privacy Policy. We are not responsible for their independent data handling practices.

6. Your Rights

Under Nepal's Individual Privacy Act, 2075, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Data Retention

Local data on your device is retained until you uninstall the app. Cloud data in Firebase Firestore is retained until you submit a deletion request. Upon a verified deletion request, your Firestore data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

Your personal income/expense tracker data (see section 1) exists only on your device, so it is retained only until you uninstall the app or delete it within the app - there is no separate cloud deletion request to make for it.

Note: Uninstalling the app removes local data only. Firebase cloud data requires a separate deletion request.

8. Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you through the app or via email within a reasonable timeframe, and take immediate steps to contain and mitigate the breach.

9. Children's Privacy

Hamro Khataa is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or applicable laws. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
